The framework that might finally fix documentation

Diátaxis argues that tutorials, how-to guides, reference material, and explanation are four fundamentally different things that most writers conflate. Treating them as one mode is why so much documentation fails the reader before page two.

The four-quadrant model is simple enough to fit on an index card. It distinguishes by what the reader is doing: learning, working, consulting, or understanding. Each demands a different kind of writing. Mixing them produces prose that is technically accurate and practically useless.
