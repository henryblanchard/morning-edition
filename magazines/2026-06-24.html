The Field That Gives Mass to Everything

François Englert, who died on 22 June 2026 at the age of 94, co-discovered the mechanism by which elementary particles acquire mass. The theory, published in 1964, predicted the existence of a boson that the physics community would spend fifty years and billions of dollars searching for. They found it at CERN in 2012, and Englert shared the Nobel Prize the following year. He was a quiet, methodical man who changed what we know about the structure of reality.
