Meta unlocks its abandoned hardware for the people who own it

Meta has pushed a firmware update to its discontinued Portal smart-display line that enables Android Debug Bridge access -- effectively handing back hardware the company abandoned to its owners. Queen bees emerge from chemically distinct wax

A new study finds that queen-cell wax has a measurably distinct composition from worker-cell wax: lower melting point, different fatty-acid profile, shaped by secretions from the nurse bees attending the cell. It is the kind of finding that starts as a footnote in a materials journal and ends, three decades later, as the foundation of something large nobody anticipated.

Latent agents

Teaching a single model to simulate multi-agent debate inside itself

A preprint proposing a post-training procedure that teaches a single model to run multi-agent debate internally -- multiple perspectives in latent space, no separate model instances, no coordination overhead. Early results suggest improved reasoning without the infrastructure cost of actual multi-agent systems. Whether this scales is an open question; that it works at all is worth tracking closely.
