The copper key to Alzheimer's

A drug designed to ferry copper across the blood-brain barrier has restored memory in Alzheimer's animal models and cleared amyloid plaques in the process. The finding reopens a decades-old hypothesis about copper dysregulation and cognitive decline, one that largely fell out of fashion when amyloid became the dominant target. Copper biology may have been quietly load-bearing all along.
