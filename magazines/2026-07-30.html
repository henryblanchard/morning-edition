Science.org investigated the research publication patterns of the major AI startups and found a dramatic retreat from open academic contribution. Companies once prolific in publishing, including the labs that produced the foundational papers of the current generation, have significantly reduced what they share.

The shift from open science to competitive secrecy is accelerating at precisely the moment the field is most consequential. If you teach AI or build on AI, the knowledge gap between what labs know and what the rest of us can read is widening every quarter.
