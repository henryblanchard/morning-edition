End of an Era  ·  Crowdsourcing
vii
The End of Human Microlabour

Amazon Mechanical Turk, the crowdsourcing platform that quietly powered a decade of AI training data, academic psychology studies, and annotation pipelines, shuts down on 30 September 2026. Its closure is not a surprise; it is a signal. For years, researchers used MTurk to gather the labelled data that trained the first generation of supervised models.

What replaces it is a combination of synthetic data generation and specialised annotation vendors with far more rigorous quality controls. The era when anyone with an internet connection could contribute to AI training, however modestly, is ending quietly. The machines have learnt enough.

10
A Cancer That Has Long
Resisted Treatment Gets
a Targeted Drug

The FDA has approved the first targeted therapy for metastatic pancreatic cancer, a disease that has remained almost entirely resistant to the precision-medicine gains that transformed treatment of other solid tumours over the past two decades.

Unlike chemotherapy, which attacks dividing cells indiscriminately, the new drug class targets a specific molecular pathway found in a subset of patients. Survival data from the trial suggests meaningful benefit where previously none existed.

Pancreatic cancer's five-year survival rate sits below 12 percent globally. Any approved targeted option marks a genuine inflection. The subset of patients who qualify is small; the proof-of-concept it establishes is not.
