The Model Cannot Tell You From the Document You Gave It

A new theoretical framing for one of AI's most persistent attack vectors: prompt injection works because models have no reliable way to distinguish between instructions from the operator and content from the environment. The paper names this structural ambiguity "role confusion" and argues it cannot be patched away. For anyone building AI products that handle user-submitted content, this framing should be informing your threat model now, not after the first incident.
