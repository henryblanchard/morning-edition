The implications for how AI systems will develop each other are only beginning to be understood.

Mouth It, Don't Say It

Two Georgia Tech students have built smart glasses that decode silent mouthed words into text, at the speed of speech and without any sound. The device reads radio-frequency signals from inside the mouth; you can give it commands in a crowded room and no one notices. From a YC batch, this is either niche accessibility hardware or the first prototype of invisible human-computer interaction.

A New State of Magnetic Matter, Confirmed

Stanford's SLAC facility has demonstrated experimental proof of altermagnetism, a magnetic phase predicted theoretically but never definitively observed. Unlike ferromagnets and antiferromagnets, altermagnets carry no net magnetic moment while still breaking time-reversal symmetry in ways that could matter for future electronics and spintronics.
