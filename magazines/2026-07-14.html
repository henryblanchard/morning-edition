A Language Designed by AI, for AI to Write

Jacquard is a programming language whose design was generated by asking an AI to analyze the abstract syntax trees of several existing languages and invent a new one. Content-addressed semantics, explicit side-effect signatures, and replay-able execution fall out of the result. Named after the loom that prefigured Babbage's Analytical Engine.
