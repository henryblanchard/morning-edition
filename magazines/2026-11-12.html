The First Molecule That Could Copy Itself

Quanta Magazine revisits the RNA world hypothesis -- the idea that life began not with DNA or proteins but with RNA, a molecule that can both carry information and act as an enzyme. New experiments are closing in on plausible conditions under which RNA self-replicated without any biological machinery to help. If confirmed, the origin of life reduces, in principle, to chemistry.

of paint splatters are valid Perl programs

A 2019 SIGBOVIK paper calculates that 93% of randomly generated pixel-art noise is syntactically valid Perl. Perl's grammar is so permissive that almost any sequence of characters parses as code. This is either a devastating critique of a language's design or a beautiful argument for expressive ambiguity; the community still cannot agree which.
