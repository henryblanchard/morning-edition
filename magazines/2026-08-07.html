A Solar Fluid Instability, Hidden for Decades, Finally Caught

The Kelvin-Helmholtz instability, where fluid layers at different speeds shear and form wave vortices, was long theorised on the Sun's surface but never directly observed until the NSF Inouye Solar Telescope caught it. The same physics governs ocean waves, atmospheric jet streams, and Saturn's cloud bands; finding it in the solar corona may revise how the Sun sheds energy into space

Coinbase spent a year tearing down and reconstructing its engineering interview process after AI coding tools made take-home exercises and syntax puzzles useless as signals.
