How far our noise has actually travelled.

A clean visualisation traces every signal humanity has broadcast outward, the radio bubble now roughly a century deep, and quietly deflates the romance of it. The honest finding is that our transmissions thin into static long before they reach anywhere interesting, which reframes the old question of whether anyone out there could hear us at all. It is less a story about aliens than about how small a very loud species still is, measured against the distances it is shouting into.
