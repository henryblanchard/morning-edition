Please, Thank You, and Whether It Actually Helps

A 2025 paper tested whether adding polite language to prompts changes the accuracy of large language models. It does, in some contexts, in both directions. The effect sizes are modest but real. If you teach people how to talk to AI, this paper belongs in your syllabus. The implications are practical, not philosophical: what you say and how you say it both matter, even to a model that does not feel offended.

Stress Makes It Harder to Connect the Dots

New research in Science Advances shows that stress disrupts the hippocampus's ability to link related memories and draw inferences from overlapping events. Under pressure, you become less capable of seeing how things connect. The mechanism is neurological, not motivational. Knowing that does not make the board meeting easier, but it does explain quite a lot.
