John Deere Farmers Win the Right to Fix Their Own Machines

Under a new FTC settlement, John Deere owners will gain the right to repair their own agricultural equipment using independent mechanics and parts. The decade-long fight pitted farmers against one of the most powerful companies in the world. The outcome has implications far beyond tractors: it sets a precedent for who controls what you own once the software is inside it. When a machine needs the manufacturer's permission to run, ownership becomes conditional.

Teaching a Five-Year-Old in Under a Second

Ello built a real-time AI reading tutor for children aged 4 to 9. The challenge was not just building a capable model: effective teaching at conversation speed required replacing the standard tool-use loop entirely. Their system uses a streaming interpreter for immediate action while a separate planner reasons one step ahead. The result is a tutor that reads a child's learning state in real time. The design decisions here are worth studying by anyone building AI-assisted education.
