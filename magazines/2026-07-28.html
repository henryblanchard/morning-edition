Three theses on the literacy crisis

Trevor Aleo lays out three arguments about why reading ability has deteriorated: that the crisis is real, that it is structural rather than cultural, and that the solutions being offered are mostly theatrical. The piece is the kind of careful, specific argumentation that is increasingly rare in education writing.

Worth reading if you teach, lecture, or design learning materials. The gap between what students can read and what we assume they can read keeps widening, and most curriculum design has not caught up. The implications for AI-augmented learning are significant.
